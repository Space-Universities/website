UK Space Conference 2025 – Student Research Symposium

The UK Space Conference is back for 2025 with a new venue, a host of new conference features, and another international line up of expert speakers. A lot has happened since the last event in Belfast 2023 and the sectors overriding emotion is excitement as they prepare to unpack what it all means for the future of space business, defence, and research.

Space Universities Network and the UK Space Agency have teamed up to provide an opportunity for Undergraduate and Postgraduate Taught students to present their research at the UK Space Conference. This 2 day event will be held in Manchester on 16th and 17th July 2025, and is the premier opportunity to connect with industry, share new ideas and shape the future of space in the UK. The interest in space in the UK is growing, and you are a part of this future!

The future of the Space Industry in the UK is more than just Science, Technology, Engineering and Maths – to develop our industry to its forefront additionally requires people familiar with Policy and Law, Humanities and Art, and Economics and Business. In light of this ambition, applications are welcomed from everyone with a passion for space, conducting research for the future!
