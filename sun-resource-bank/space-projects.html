In the final or penultimate year of their degree, most students have to complete a lengthy individual and/or project, and space-related topics are rarely available.

Spaceprojects provides the framework for students to do their project remotely with a space organisation, and advertise projects proposed by space organisations to students nationwide on a rolling basis.

This scheme has been created out of a partnership between UKSEDS and SUN to give students this crucial experience.
