Question Bank

The Question Bank can be found behind the JISC login.

This bank contains example exam questions from a range of universities, mostly centered around space systems.

Accessing Question Bank

1. Navigate to the JISC list

2. Click here to log into JISC. If it is the first time signing in, you might need to set up an account. Alternatively, you should be able to sign in with your university email account.

3. On the right hand side, navigate to Files Area.

4. You will then enter the file store, with the Question Bank inside the appropriate folder.
