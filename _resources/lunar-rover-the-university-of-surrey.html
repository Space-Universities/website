The University of Surrey ‘Electronics and Amateur Radio Society’ (EARS) sent a team to compete in the UKSEDS Lunar Rover competition to design and build a rover to achieve an objective set by the organisers. This was an extra-curricular project run by students who developed the rover over a academic year and competed in a final test day.
