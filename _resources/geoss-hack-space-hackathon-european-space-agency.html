The European Space Agency (ESA) have run a Space Hackathon in partnership with the Group on Earth Observations (GEO). 30 participants split into 6 teams had 30 hours to create an open-source solution to 1 of 6 challenges focused around meeting the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) using Earth Observation data from the GEOSS portal.
