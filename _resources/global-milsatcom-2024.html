Join the Future of Space Exploration: Graduate Space Careers Forum at Global MilSatCom

Are you passionate about space and eager to explore exciting career opportunities? The Graduate Space Careers Forum is your gateway to discovering non-traditional career paths in the rapidly growing space sector. Held on Day 2 of the prestigious Global MilSatCom Conference, which is the largest gathering of MilSatCom professionals, and it is now returning for its 26th year.  This forum is designed to inspire and equip the next generation of space professionals.

With 150 undergraduate and postgraduate students from across the UK, this forum tackles the critical skills shortage in the space industry. This is a rare chance to connect with industry experts, gain insights into the latest trends, and explore diverse roles in the booming UK space sector.

Why Attend?

  • Learn about emerging career opportunities in space
  • Gain valuable skills and knowledge for your future
  • Network with industry leaders and peers
  • Be part of the solution to shape the future of space technology

Eligibility:

  • Free to attend for students at UK universities
  • UK university students must present valid student and legal ID

How to Register: Click here to apply. If successful, you’ll receive a confirmation email from SAE Media Group within two weeks.
