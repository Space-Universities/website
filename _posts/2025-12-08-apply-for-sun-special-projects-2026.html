This call has now closed.

SUN is pleased to announce that we will be including Special Projects from our members in our bid for UKSA funding in 2026. This is an oppertunity for SUN members to apply for up to £25,000 of funding to develop and deliver content for teaching space and space related topics.

After the success of SUN's previous special projects Rocketlab!, Rocket Safety with Dr Peter Shaw and The Open University's Mars Yard we are very excited to see the creativity and expertise the SUN community will bring to SUN Special Projects 2026.

How to apply

Deadline for submissions is 18th January 2026

Shortlist will be announced 27th January 2026
